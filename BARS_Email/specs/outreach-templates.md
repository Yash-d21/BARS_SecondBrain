## Style rules (locked)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **No em dash** | Never use `—` or `–`. Use a period, comma, or short new sentence. |
| **Simple words** | Short sentences. Founder language. No corporate fluff. |
| **Bold keywords** | Bold the trackable words below so drafts and replies are easy to scan. |
| **No website / location** | Never name their URL, domain, city, or area in the body. Talk about what they **do** / ship / offer. |
| **Keep the middle voice** | Pain + stay-on + "That's exactly what we do at **BARS**" stays. Only the intro changes. |
| **Think before the pain** | Match pain to how they actually make money. Do not invent **GST** for session-only clinics. |
| **Length** | Body **75 to 120 words**. Signature is mailbox/SMTP, not in the draft. |

Use site and city only in the research note. Not in the email.

### Pain fit (read before drafting)

| Business type | Prefer pain | Do not force |
|---------------|-------------|--------------|
| Session / consultation clinic (homeopathy, therapy, coaching) | **books** / **month-end** / cash vs sessions | **GST** (unless they clearly sell medicines or retail products) |
| Clinic + pharmacy / remedies for sale | clinic **books** + **GST** on product sales | Generic "all clinics need GST" |
| D2C / SKU / wellness products | **COGS**, **GST**, inventory cash | Session language |
| Studio / membership | **membership** vs **payroll** | Product **GST** unless they also sell goods |
| Software / services | **books**, **runway**, **filings** as fits stage | Inventory / SKU language |

**Rule:** If research does not show product sales, do not mention **GST**. Use books / month-end / cash clarity instead.

### Bold these keywords (when you use them)

**Interaction:** company name if natural, **product**, **hiring**, **clinic**, **studio**, what they sell  
**Pain:** **books**, **month-end**, **GST**, **filings**, **compliance**, **runway**, **payroll**, **HR**, **MIS**, **investor-ready**, **COGS**, **membership**, **SKU**  
**Close:** **BARS**, **15 minutes**

In drafts use markdown: `**books**`. SMTP/HTML should render as bold.

## What to fix vs keep

| Part | Verdict | Rule |
|------|---------|------|
| Intro (URL, city, CIN, inbox dump) | **Bad** | Replace with what they do + optional question |
| Pain (`Your pain is…`) | **Keep** | One clear pain in that voice |
| Stay-on (`You should stay on…`) | **Keep** | Founder focus vs back-office |
| `That's exactly what we do at BARS` | **Keep** | Short close. No long about-us / service dump |
| CTA (Cal.com) | **Keep** | Locked |

## Master plate (5 beats)

```
1. INTERACTION  = what they do (+ optional short question). No URL. No city.
2. PAIN         = Your pain is X. Then 1 to 2 short lines on how it shows up.
3. STAY ON      = You should stay on Y. Back-office should not wait.
4. CLOSE        = That's exactly what we do at BARS.
5. CTA          = Cal.com discovery call
```

### Skeleton

```
Hi {{first_name}},

{{interaction}}

Your pain is {{pain_name}}. {{pain_how_it_shows}}.

You should stay on {{founder_focus}}. {{back_office}} should not wait for a quiet week.

That's exactly what we do at **BARS**.

If it's relevant, happy to chat for **15 minutes**: https://cal.com/anirudh-reddy
```

### Slot rules

| Slot | Must | Must not |
|------|------|----------|
| **Interaction** | What they do / sell / ship + optional question | Their website, city, CIN, board, phone |
| **Pain** | `Your pain is…` + how it shows up | Multiple pains, fake urgency, em dashes |
| **Stay on** | Founder focus vs one back-office job | Capability menu |
| **Close** | `That's exactly what we do at **BARS**.` | Long about-us paragraph |
| **CTA** | Cal.com once | Second link, pricing, deck |

## Subject line rules

**No first name. No company name.** Subject is the pain or question only. Name and company live in the body greeting / intro.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Pain / question only | First name |
| 3 to 7 words | Company / brand name |
| Lowercase OK | ALL CAPS / !!! |
| Period or comma if you need a break | Em dash |
| | Website or city |

**Default patterns:**
- `who owns month-end?`
- `clinic month-end`
- `first batch books ready?`
- `GST vs product clock`
- `books before the raise`
- `membership vs payroll`
- `cash clarity at this stage`
