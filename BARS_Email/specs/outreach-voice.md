## Tone

Direct, founder-friendly, execution-first. Not corporate jargon. Not aggressive sales.

## Style (cold body)

- **No em dash** (`—` or `–`). Use a period or comma.
- Keep sentences short and simple.
- **Bold** trackable keywords (`**books**`, `**GST**`, `**BARS**`, `**15 minutes**`, etc.). Full list: [[outreach-templates#Bold these keywords]].
- **Never** put their website, URL, domain, city, or area in the email. That looks automated. Open on what they do / sell / ship.

## Use (pick one per email)

- Hyderabad-based execution partner (not slide-deck consultants)
- End-to-end: accounting through branding
- Works with founders from idea stage through scale
- Custom by size, industry, stage

## Pain points to reference

- Founder drowning in ops while building product
- No finance/HR/compliance structure at idea/seed
- GST, payroll, reporting falling behind during growth
- CFO-level thinking without full-time hire
- Books not investor-ready before fundraising

## Avoid

- "Best consulting firm" superlatives
- Long capability lists
- Generic "we help businesses grow"
- Placeholder testimonials (site still has placeholders)
- Lorem ipsum / fake case studies
- Pricing or guarantees in cold email
