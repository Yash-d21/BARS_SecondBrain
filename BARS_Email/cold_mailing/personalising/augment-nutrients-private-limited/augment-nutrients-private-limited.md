## What they do

**Product:** Animal feed additive / nutrition company. Site positions EnzoMeal (soy-based meal technology licensed for Asia Pacific from Ohio Soybean Council / research partners; aims to remove anti-nutritional factors from conventional soybean meal) and NutriPro (feed supplement: proteins, enzymes, amino acids, vitamins for farmed animals).

**Model:** Manufacturing / wholesale of feed additives and supplements (MCA activity often coded as wholesale trading). Sustainability narrative vs fish meal / fish oil.

**Customers:** Livestock / aquaculture feed channel (inferred from product copy). Exact customer list unknown.

**Trademarks (QuickCompany):** “Augment Nutrients” and “Manafeed” (Class 31, animal food) registered ~2021.

1. **Scaling agri wholesale + bank charges** — ₹12 Cr+ revenue with SBI/BoI charges implies working-capital, inventory, and GST discipline as volume grows.
2. **Group / related-party adjacency** — Shared directors with Matrix Seafoods / Svada Exports: related-party, transfer pricing, and consolidated MIS hygiene.
3. **Registered vs ops address** — Madhapur ROC address vs Kondapur Hive Space ops; GST/place-of-business hygiene as they scale.
4. **DPIIT Scaling stage** — Benefits documentation, board MIS, and lender-ready reporting without a full-time CFO.
