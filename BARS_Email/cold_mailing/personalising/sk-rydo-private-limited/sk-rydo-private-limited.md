## What they do

**Brand Hailo** operated by SK RYDO PRIVATE LIMITED: ride-hailing / mobility marketplace connecting riders with independent driver-partners (bike taxi, auto, city cab, rentals, outstation). Claims upfront pricing, verified drivers, live tracking, SOS; zero commission messaging on bike taxi and auto (about page). Platform states it is **not** the transport carrier; drivers are independent contractors ([terms](https://hailoindia.com/terms-and-conditions)).
