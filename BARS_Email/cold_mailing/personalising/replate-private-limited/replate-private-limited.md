## What they do

**Product:** Surplus-food marketplace (“Too Good To Go” style for India). Partner bakeries/cafés list end-of-day unsold items as discounted **Surprise Bags** (₹99–249 for food typically worth ₹300–600). Pickup-only at the bakery counter (no delivery).

**Model:** Marketplace / revenue share on surplus bags; partners reply on WhatsApp with bag count ~3h before close; customers claim via WhatsApp group or app; payout to bakery within 24 hours (partners page claims).

**Customers:** Urban consumers (students, professionals, families); partners = licensed premium bakeries/cafés first.

**Cities / coverage:**
- **Hyderabad** — live across Madhapur, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Kokapet, Nanakaramguda, Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Himayatnagar, Sainikpuri, Tirumalgiri, Aramgarh, etc.
- **Bengaluru** — live nightly since **1 Aug 2026** (WhatsApp-first; HYD has iOS + Android apps).

**Named partners (examples):** Bikanervala, Bakelore, Brown Bear Bakers, The Hole in the Wall Cafe (HYD); Big Bean Cafe, Brown Butter Co., 7th Heaven, Gulheera Coffee (BLR).

## Pain hooks for BARS (Virtual CFO / finance–HR–compliance–ops)

1. **Marketplace settlements & partner payouts** — nightly bag sales, 24h bakery payouts, multi-city cash/UPI reconciliation, refunds policy — needs clean books + payment ops discipline as volume scales from 60→hundreds of bags/day.
2. **Very new Pvt Ltd (Jul 2026)** — first-year MCA compliance, GST registration/returns, FSSAI adjacency for marketplace claims, DPIIT benefits documentation.
3. **Two-city expansion (HYD→BLR→3rd city)** — entity structure, state GST, payroll/contractor for BD hires, unit economics / runway (both founders are finance-literate; speak their language: cashflow, cohort LTV, partner take-rate).
4. **Capital raise readiness** — EarlyTraction + clear metrics; Virtual CFO for MIS, burn, partner receivables, and investor-ready reporting without a full-time hire.
