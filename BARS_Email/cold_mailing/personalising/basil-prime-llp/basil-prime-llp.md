## Pain hooks for BARS (Virtual CFO / finance–HR–compliance–ops)

1. **Very new LLP (Dec 2025) + EarlyTraction** — first-year LLP compliance, GST, partner contribution tracking, and books while chasing first construction / logistics wins.
2. **Three P&Ls under one brand** — infra projects, software retainers, and freight margins need cost centres / MIS, not one lump ledger.
3. **Turnkey / works-contract cash** — advances, retentions, vendor payments, and subcontractors typical of construction EaaS.
4. **Lean team (1–10)** — Virtual CFO cheaper than hiring full-time finance before revenue stabilises.
