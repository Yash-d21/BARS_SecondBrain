## Pain hooks for BARS (Virtual CFO / finance–HR–compliance–ops)

1. **Hospital finance complexity** — insurance empanelment, claim AR days, package pricing vs open billing, doctor revenue share / visiting consultant settlements, pharmacy/consumables inventory.
2. **Debt-backed CapEx** — ~₹4.75 Cr charge; needs covenant tracking, cashflow forecasting, lender reporting — Virtual CFO sweet spot.
3. **Compliance stack** — NABH/ISO maintenance, clinical documentation, MCA filings (AGM/AOC history), GST, labour for clinical + non-clinical staff, biomedical waste / PCPNDT if applicable later.
4. **Growth from single specialty to multi-dept** — costing by department, MIS for Rajesh’s P&L/brand building, payroll vs retainer mix HR.
5. **Founder-clinician + professional manager** — Dr Samhitha clinical lead; Rajesh commercial — BARS can sit between clinical owners and professional management on finance/ops.
