**Go-to-market:** Entering via **retrofit conversion of existing petrol scooters** (affordable hybrid mobility), while developing **purpose-built hybrid vehicles**.

**Traction / prototype (self-reported on LinkedIn About):**
- First prototype **built and validated**

**Customers (intended):** Two-wheeler riders seeking range/performance without pure-EV compromise; retrofit market for existing petrol scooters (India-relevant).

**Cities:** Based in Hyderabad (Secunderabad / Moulali registered). No multi-city retail footprint found.
